How to Calculate and Optimize EOQ and Reorder Point for Maximum Cash Flow

Inventory management is the silent engine of your cash flow. Holding too much stock traps your working capital, while holding too little leads to missed sales and customer churn. This guide provides the exact mathematical framework to balance these risks.

1. Calculating Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

EOQ identifies the optimal order volume that minimizes the sum of ordering and holding costs.

Formula: EOQ = SQRT((2 Annual Demand Order Cost) / Holding Cost per Unit)

Defining Holding Cost per Unit

Many business owners underestimate holding costs. To get a realistic figure, you must factor in the cost of capital (WACC). Use this table structure in Excel (A1: Cost Item, B1: Annual Amount, C1: Calculation):

| Cost Item | Annual Amount | Excel Formula / Logic |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Warehouse Rent & Utilities | 12,000 | Total Annual Rent / Avg Units |

| Insurance |2,000 | Total Annual Premium / Avg Units |

| Cost of Capital (WACC 10%) | 10,000 | Inventory Value * 0.10 |

| Total Holding Cost | 24,000 | Sum of above / Total Units |

2. Vaka Analizi: A vs C Grubu Ürünler

Not all items require the same replenishment strategy. Below is how EOQ shifts based on demand velocity:

| Product Type | Annual Demand | Holding Cost/Unit | EOQ (Optimal Order) |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| A-Group (Best Seller) | 20,000 units | 0.50 | 2,000 units |

| C-Group (Slow Mover) | 1,000 units |2.00 | 100 units |

Insight: A-Group items require frequent, smaller orders to keep cash flowing, while C-Group items should be ordered in bulk to minimize administrative ordering costs.

3. Dynamic Reorder Point (ROP) with Safety Stock

Never trigger an order based on intuition. Use the ROP formula:

ROP = (Average Daily Usage * Lead Time in Days) + Safety Stock

Calculating Safety Stock (Z-Score Method)

For a 95% service level (Z = 1.65), calculate your safety buffer based on demand volatility:

Safety Stock = 1.65 STDEV.P(Range) SQRT(Lead Time)

Note: The 'Range' in STDEV.P must represent the last 30-60 days of daily sales data to reflect current market trends.

4. Implementation in Excel

Set up your tracker with these columns:

  • A1: Item Name | B1: Annual Demand | C1: Order Cost | D1: Holding Cost
  • E1: EOQ | F1: Daily Usage | G1: Lead Time | H1: Safety Stock | I1: ROP
  • Formula for H2: `=1.65 STDEV.P(B10:B40) SQRT(G2)` (B10:B40 is your 30-day sales history).
