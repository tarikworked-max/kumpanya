For cafe owners, the difference between profit and loss often hides in the trash bin. If you are not tracking your 'Food Cost Percentage' (FCP) weekly, you are likely losing 5-10% of your revenue to invisible waste, over-portioning, and inefficient inventory turnover.

Calculating Your Food Cost Percentage in Excel

To calculate your FCP, you need your Beginning Inventory, Purchases, and Ending Inventory for a specific period. Use the following structure in your Excel sheet:

  1. Beginning Inventory (A2): Value of stock at the start of the week.
  2. Purchases (B2): Total cost of new ingredients bought.
  3. Ending Inventory (C2): Value of remaining stock at the end of the week.
  4. Total Food Sales (D2): Revenue generated from food items.

The Formula:

In cell E2, calculate your Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): `=(A2 + B2 - C2)`

In cell F2, calculate your FCP: `= (E2 / D2) * 100`

Identifying Waste vs. Consumption

Waste is not just spoilage; it is also over-portioning. To track waste, create a 'Waste Log' sheet in your Excel file. Set up a column for 'Item Name', 'Quantity Wasted', and 'Unit Cost'.

Use this formula to calculate the financial impact of waste: `=SUMPRODUCT(B2:B10, C2:C10)` where B is the quantity and C is the unit cost. If your weekly waste exceeds 2% of your total purchases, it is time to standardize your recipes.
